# Bihar Land-Record Availability Check

**Scope:** Determine whether official Bihar public portals expose a specific Amity-adjacent parcel, map sheet, plot information, holder basis, or ULPIN for the supplied Patna → Danapur → Danapur Circle → Mauza 12 candidate path. This note does not assert that the supplied administrative path corresponds to an Amity parcel.

## Official entry points

The Bihar Revenue and Land Reforms Department lists BiharBhumi and Bhu-Manchitra among its public land-record services. The official Bihar BhuNaksha URL serves a location-driven public map interface with district, subdivision, circle, mauza, survey type, map instance, sheet number, and Plot Info controls. [1] [2]

## Availability result — 24 August 2026

The BhuNaksha landing page initially exposed the public workflow and a Patna district option. Before the Patna → Danapur → Mauza 12 selections could be completed, the official host closed the browser connection. No map sheet, plot number, polygon, holder/owner basis, ULPIN, or parcel reference was obtained.

The official BiharBhumi site also closed the browser connection. A text-only retrieval returned only the government portal's landing content and did not expose a public Jamabandi or parcel result for the candidate path. Consequently, neither official portal produced a specific record that could be connected to Amity-adjacent source footprints during this session.

## Evidence decision

No data was imported or displayed in the application. The supplied location path remains an unverified candidate only. A future check must complete the official portal selection flow and capture the exact displayed Plot Info before any parcel geometry, holder basis, or ULPIN can be considered for a source-footprint link.
